Entering

You'll be given a passcode you can use to unlock the front door during your stay.

The first door you'll see is a white door with a large flower design. This is an unlocked security door. Go through this door and you'll be in the entrance and see the front door.

The front door has a keycode lock. Wave your hand in front of it for the numbers to show up. Type in your code and then tap the area where the lock icon is on the keypad.

To lock the door when you leave: tap the lock icon twice.

House Rules

I would really appreciate it if you could follow the house rules:

  • No smoking inside, but you may smoke outside on the roof deck.
  • Our neighbors are nice people — please keep the noise level low.

Amenities

Shared Areas

You have access to the rooftop deck and the kitchen.

Heating / Air Conditioning

You can adjust the temperature using one of the two remotes. Set it to heat or cool by setting the icon to snowflake or sun. Use the up and down arrows to adjust. Although there are two heaters, they both have to be at the same hot/cold setting.

Laundry

You also have access to the washer and dryer on the second floor.

Check Out

Please make sure all trash is in the trash can and towels are gathered in one pile.

Recommended Restaurants

🚶 Walkable 🚊 Light rail 🚗 Car / Uber 🥡 Order-in

Thai

Noi Thai 🚗🥡 — try their panang curry, pineapple fried rice, and garlic chicken.

Pho

Venison Pho 🚗🥡 — try their beef brisket pho.

Dumplings

Din Tai Fung 🚗 — the most famous dumpling place in Seattle. A little pricey and may require a reservation.

Tacos

Tacos Chukis 🚶 — street style tacos, simple and delicious, best with a beer and friends.
Carmelo's Tacos 🥡 — perfect for delivery.
Rocket Tacos 🚶 — street tacos in a quieter part of Seattle, walkable. Best choice for a good margarita.

American

Dick's Drive In 🚶 — a historic burger joint. Low cost; one is on Broadway.

Fancy

MBar 🚗 (South Lake Union) — about $200 for two, but you'll dine atop a high-rise with a view of Lake Union. Outdoor space with heaters and blankets. Reservation needed.

Breakfast

Portage Bay Cafe 🚗 — reservation required. The most popular brunch in Seattle.
Lost Lake Cafe and Lounge 🚶 — traditional breakfast, pretty good. Bonus for being in Cap Hill.

Desserts

Hello Robin 🚶 — ice cream sandwiches with artisan ice cream and fresh-baked cookies.
Midnight Cookie Co 🥡 — the only dessert place that delivers late at night.
Cinnaholic 🥡 — cinnamon rolls with all the toppings.
Hot Cakes 🚶 — molten chocolate cakes with ice cream. Also has the best grilled cheese with tomato soup. Various dietary options.
Molly Moon 🚶 — artisan ice cream.
Salt and Straw 🚶 — artisan ice cream.
Frankie & Jo's 🚶 — vegan artisan ice cream.

Coffee Shops

Volunteer Park Cafe 🚶 — good coffee and food, cute homey atmosphere. Walk down 17th Ave E into the quiet neighborhood, stop for coffee, then stroll through Volunteer Park.
Bakery Nouveau 🚶 — French bakery with pastries, sandwiches, and basic coffee. Grab food and head to the roof deck.
Nuflours Bakery 🚶 — gluten-free bakery, so good I didn't realize it was gluten-free! Unique artisan baked goods.
Ada's Technical Books 🚶 — technical bookstore and coffee shop on 15th. Coffee, treats, books, and brain teasers.
Victrola Coffee & Cafe Ladro 🚶 — both decent, what you'd typically expect.
Overcast Coffee Co 🚶 — accessed from the street, also part of the medical center on 15th. Great sandwiches and friendly people.

Entertainment & Nightlife

Things to Do at Least Once

Space Needle and Big Wheel 🚗 — 'nuff said.
Columbia Tower Observatory 🚗 — Seattle's tallest building.
Pike Place Market 🚗 — multi-story market.
Woodland Park Zoo & Seattle Aquarium 🚗
Bainbridge Island 🚗 — take the ferry; the ride itself is an experience and Bainbridge has a cute downtown.

Karaoke

RockBox 🚶 — go-to for karaoke rooms; popular songs, Capitol Hill nightlife, good drinks.
Seattle's Best Karaoke (SBK) 🚗 — best sound system; bring your own drinks/snacks; sing anything from YouTube.
Crescent Lounge 🚶 — Seattle's oldest straight-friendly gay karaoke bar. Lively crowd, mixed talent.
Hula Hula 🚶 — similar to Crescent Lounge but larger. Very lively. Close enough to try both.

Dancing

Havana 🚶 — Hip Hop, packed, great dancing.
Kremwerk 🚗 — industrial music.
Chop Suey 🚶 — Hip Hop, Alt Rock, Pop (check website for schedule).
Neighbors 🚶 — only club open until 4am. LGBTQ+ club.
Sarajevo 🚗 — Latin and Hip Hop.
ELAV8 🚗 — Latin and Hip Hop. Both Latin clubs are in Belltown — visit both in one night.

Drinks +

Hopvine Pub 🚶 — small pub on 15th; live music, open mic, comedy nights.
Mox Boarding House 🚗 — pub and game shop; great for board game nights.
Pie Bar 🚶 — cute hole-in-the-wall cocktail bar with pies made on site.
Bathtub Gin and Co. 🚗 — accessed from an alley, Seattle's best-kept secret cocktail bar.
Elysian Brewery 🚶 — famous Seattle brewery.
Redhook Brewery 🚶 — lots of outdoor seating, perfect on a hot day.

Sports Events

Lumen Field 🚊 — Seahawks (NFL) and Sounders (MLS).
T-Mobile Park 🚊 — Mariners (MLB).
Climate Pledge Arena 🚊 — Kraken (NHL) and Storm (WNBA).
Husky Stadium 🚊 — UW Huskies football and other UW athletics.

Outdoor

Kayaking on Lake Union 🚗 — rent a kayak and check out the boat houses.
Greenlake 🚗 — nice walk with restaurants nearby.
Volunteer Park 🚶 — large well-kept park, plus the Asian Arts Museum, an old water tower observation deck, a conservatory, and Bruce Lee's grave.
Bruce Lee's Grave 🚶 — in a cemetery next to Volunteer Park. Especially nice in spring during cherry blossom bloom.
UW & UW Cherry Blossoms 🚗 — beautiful campus and historic buildings. Famous cherry blossoms in spring.
Gas Works Park 🚗 — featured in 10 Things I Hate About You. Decent view of downtown.
Alki Beach 🚗 — sandy beach in West Seattle (need a car). Bonfires, beach volleyball, rollerblading.

Shopping

University Village 🚗 — best shopping center in the area. Anthropologie, Aritzia, Tommy Bahama, plus specialty shops and dining. Open air — bring an umbrella if needed.
15th Ave 🚶 — a block down, with cute shops worth checking out.
Broadway 🚶 — campy and alternative clothing. Check out Trendy Wendy and the NY Exchange.
